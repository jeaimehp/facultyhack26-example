# FacultyHack@Gateways 2026 Curriculum Project

## Project Overview

This curriculum project focuses on integrating **High-Performance Computing (HPC), Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Science Gateway resources** into an existing course to give students hands-on experience with modern research computing environments.

**Course:** *[Course Name and Number]*

The primary goal is to move students beyond working only on their local computers by introducing them to scalable computing, reproducible scientific workflows, AI-enabled research tools, and shared cyberinfrastructure. The revised curriculum will emphasize practical access to HPC and AI resources through user-friendly interfaces and Science Gateways, allowing students to focus on solving problems and analyzing data without requiring them to first become HPC system administrators.

The project will also explore how tools such as **projectEUREKA!, ACCESS resources, NAIRR resources, Python/Jupyter environments, containers, and Science Gateways** can be incorporated into assignments and course projects in ways that are accessible to students with different levels of computing experience.

## Science Gateway Resources & Technology Notes

The curriculum project explores a combination of Science Gateway, HPC, AI, and research cyberinfrastructure tools that can provide students with practical experience using the same types of computing resources increasingly used in scientific research.

### Tools Used

**projectEUREKA!** *[Add projectEUREKA! project URL]*

* **Description:** projectEUREKA! provides an environment for making HPC, AI, and research computing resources easier to deploy, access, and use. It can provide web-accessible workflows while connecting students and researchers to scalable computing infrastructure.
* **Course Use:** Students can use projectEUREKA! as an accessible entry point to HPC and AI resources for computational assignments, data analysis, scientific Python workflows, and larger course projects without requiring extensive command-line or cluster-administration experience.

**ACCESS** [https://access-ci.org/](https://access-ci.org/)

* **Description:** ACCESS provides researchers and educators with access to advanced computing, storage, visualization, and other cyberinfrastructure resources contributed by organizations across the United States.
* **Course Use:** ACCESS resources can introduce students to nationally available research cyberinfrastructure and provide a pathway for moving course projects beyond local computing environments.

**NAIRR Resources** [https://nairrpilot.org/](https://nairrpilot.org/)

* **Description:** The National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) connects researchers, educators, and students with computing, datasets, models, software, and other resources that support AI research and education.
* **Course Use:** NAIRR resources can support AI-focused course modules in which students experiment with datasets, computational resources, models, and research workflows that would be difficult to support using only classroom or personal computing resources.

**Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC)** [https://www.tacc.utexas.edu/](https://www.tacc.utexas.edu/)

* **Description:** TACC operates advanced computing systems and provides resources, training, software, data services, and expertise supporting computational research and education.
* **Course Use:** TACC materials and computing resources can be used to introduce students to HPC concepts, scientific computing workflows, remote research systems, and the role large-scale computing facilities play in modern research.

**Python / Jupyter**

* **Description:** Python and Jupyter notebooks provide an approachable environment for scientific programming, data analysis, visualization, and AI experimentation.
* **Course Use:** Jupyter notebooks can serve as the instructional bridge between traditional classroom programming assignments and execution on remote HPC or AI infrastructure. Students can develop and test workflows interactively before scaling them to larger systems.

**Containers**

* **Description:** Container technologies package applications, libraries, and dependencies into reproducible computing environments.
* **Course Use:** Students can use containers to learn reproducibility and portability while creating applications and computational workflows that can move from laptops to Science Gateways, HPC systems, and cloud or Kubernetes-based environments.

## Event Citation

This project was developed as part of **SGX3's 5th Annual FacultyHack@Gateways 2026**. FacultyHack is a hands-on program designed to empower educators across disciplines to integrate High-Performance Computing (HPC) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools directly into their curricula.

FacultyHack provides participants with technical mentorship, hands-on experience with advanced cyberinfrastructure, and an opportunity to transform what they learn into practical instructional materials that can be used with their students. The program emphasizes reducing barriers to HPC, AI, Science Gateways, and national research cyberinfrastructure while creating sustainable pathways for these technologies to reach new institutions, faculty, and students.
